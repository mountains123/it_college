Who Are We?

The "Carpathian Peaks" team consists of professional guides, mountain tourism instructors, and rescuers who know every stone on the Chornohora Ridge and every trail in the Marmarosh.

Our mission is to make mountain hiking accessible, safe, and unforgettable for everyone. We believe that the mountains heal the soul, give freedom, and teach true friendship by the evening campfire.
